## Thay đổi
> The item has been near bottom pickrate since release, and the distance between it and the next least popular Lethality item is quite far. We’re making it both more accessible and slightly stronger on its less noticeable effect, because we find that lethality users putting more of their gold into utility and progressing the team game is healthy in the long term.

  * **Cost** : 3200g ⇒ **3000g**
  * **Shaped Charge Damage** : (30 + 150% lethality) true damage ⇒ **(50 + 150% lethality) true damage**
