## Thay đổi
> Our MSI changes were effective at pulling her to the forefront of pro play for the first time in years, but unfortunately she's too performant in specific solo queue settings. Our data shows that W damage is a meaningfully larger share of damage in the tiers where she's stronger than desired, so this should help tap her down while still preserving her core fantasies. Her passive is also now better breakpointed to increase reliance on Lost Chapter in early-mid levels, nerfing her higher winrate builds that make do with less mana investment.

####  Passive - Transcendent
  * **Splinter Mana** : 20 - 255 ⇒ **20 - 199**

####  W - Force of Will
  * **Cooldown** : 12 / 11 / 10 / 9 / 8s ⇒ **13 / 12 / 11 / 10 / 9s**
