> Black Cleaver has become a Smolder / Senna item more than a core AD fighter item. We are re-adding the Melee / Ranged split on the move speed effect to buy space to buff it for AD fighters without pushing it over the top for its ranged user base.
  * **Attack Damage** : 40 ⇒ **45**
  * **Fervor Move Speed** : 20 for 2s ⇒ **[20 Melee || 10 Ranged] for 2s**
