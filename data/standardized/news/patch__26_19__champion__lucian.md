## Thay đổi
> Years ago, we added the Vigilance Passive so Lucian bot players didn't feel like their champ was being nerfed because of mid laners, which ended up being _super effective_. We think now is a good opportunity to adjust the tuning of the passive and strike a balance between both roles.

####  Passive - Vigilance
  * **Empowered On-Hit Damage** : 15 (+20% AD) ⇒ **5 (+15% AD)**

####  Q - Piercing Light
  * **Base Damage** : 80 / 115 / 150 / 185 / 220 (+100% bonus AD) ⇒ **90 / 130 / 170 / 210 / 250 (+100% bonus AD)**
