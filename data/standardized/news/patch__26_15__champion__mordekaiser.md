## Thay đổi
> We overshot last patch with the R buffs, and after re-assessing what he looks like with a meaningfully stronger ult, decided to instead revert the R buff entirely in favor of improving his ability to catch and keep opponents in his range. Accounting for the bugfix for his R, we believe these two adjustments to his E will be a net positive from 26.13 and will keep an eye on his performance.

####  E - Death's Grasp
  * **Cooldown** : 18 / 16 / 14 / 12 / 10 ⇒ **16 / 14 / 12 / 10 / 8**

####  R - Realm of Death
  * **Stolen Stats** : 13% ⇒ **10%**
  * **Bugfix** : Fixed a bug where ulting a target with health above their post-reduction max HP would lead to increased damage (up to double).
