## Thay đổi
> We think melee supports have too much of their power in roaming and all-ins and are too weak in sustained laning. In response, we're shifting the flat health to health regen on World Atlas and Runic Compass, which will benefit them more due to their higher base health regen values.

  * **Health** : 30 / 100 / 200 ⇒ **0 / 60 / 200**
  * **Health Regeneration** : 25% / 50% / 75% ⇒ **50% / 75% / 75%**
