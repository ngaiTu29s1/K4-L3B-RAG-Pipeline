## Thay đổi
> We're using a QoL change here as an opportunity to give Zaahen some love! This might read as a nerf, but Zaahen's Q lined up with his passive in such a way that it meant he always left his resurrection (and GA, Zilean R) with Q on CD, so now he's always happier. His Q2 is also a clean double of his Q1 base damage now.

####  Q - The Darkin Glaive
  * **NEW** **Cooldown Behavior** : If Q is active, it is now placed on Cooldown immediately upon being put in Resurrection
  * **Q2 Damage** : 25 / 50 / 75 / 100 / 125 (+20 / 25 / 30 / 35 / 40% bonus AD) ⇒ **30 / 60 / 90 / 120 / 150 (+20 / 25 / 30 / 35 / 40% bonus AD)**
